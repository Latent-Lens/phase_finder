Help Center Open PhaseFinder

How can we help?

Choose a topic to learn a workflow or look up a specific PhaseFinder feature.

PhaseFinder is a browser-based tool for inspecting flow cytometry .fcs files, plotting DNA-content events, and fitting cell-cycle models. Everything runs locally in your browser; files are never uploaded to a server.
